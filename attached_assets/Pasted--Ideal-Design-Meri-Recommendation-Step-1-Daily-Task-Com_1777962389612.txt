🟢 Ideal Design — Meri Recommendation
Step 1: Daily Task Completion Score
Har din ke "expected tasks" define karo based on user profile:
✅ Food Log    → 3 meals logged?      = 30 points (10 each)
✅ Water       → daily goal reach?    = 20 points
✅ Exercise    → logged?              = 20 points (rest day = auto 20)
✅ Medicine    → sirf agar scheduled  = 15 points
✅ Stress      → check-in done?       = 10 points
✅ Snacks      → food ke andar count  = no extra
Max per day = 100 points

Step 2: Smart Missing Day Logic
Ek bhi screen open kiya aur kuch bhi nahi kiya → 0%
App hi nahi khola → "No data" (average mein count na ho)

Yeh important hai — warna jo 10 din app nahi khola uska average bahut low ho jayega unfairly.

Step 3: Cumulative Average (Aapka idea ✅)
Install date se aaj tak ke "app khola" wale dino ka average
March 1 install → March 20 tak
→ 15 din app khola (5 din nahi khola)
→ In 15 dino ka average daily score = Final Active %

Aapka idea current system se bahut better hai. Jo ek cheez main change karunga:

❌ "20 mein se 20 dino ka average" → unfair for missed days

✅ "20 mein se jo 15 din app khola, unka average" → honest aur motivating
