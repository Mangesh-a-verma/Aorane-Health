Getting Started
Onboarding Process

Get Your Organization Live
in Less Than a Week

Aorane's onboarding is completely self-serve — no IT infrastructure needed, no installation required. Sign up, configure, share codes, and your employees are tracking health within days.
1
Day 1 — Register Your Organization (5 minutes)
Visit business.aorane.com → Register with org name, type (corporate/hospital/gym etc.), contact email, GSTIN, and admin password. Receive welcome email with org code immediately.
⏱️ 5 minutes
2
Day 1 — Choose Your Plan & Pay
Select Max (₹199/seat) or Pro (₹249/seat) plan. Pay securely via Razorpay — UPI, credit card, net banking all accepted. Get GST invoice instantly.
💳 Razorpay Secure Payment
3
Day 2 — Generate Enrollment Codes
From your dashboard, create enrollment codes for each department or batch. Set expiry dates and seat limits per code. Share via email, WhatsApp, or printed QR code.
🔑 Unlimited Codes
4
Day 2–4 — Employee Enrollment
Employees download Aorane app (iOS/Android), register with their mobile number, and enter the enrollment code. They're automatically linked to your organization. No IT setup needed.
📱 Self-serve by employees
5
Day 5+ — Monitor & Engage
Your dashboard starts showing real data as employees track. Send your first announcement. View health score distribution. Identify any high-stress employees for support.
📊 Live from Day 1
🚀
No IT Setup Required
100% cloud-based. No servers, no VPN, no enterprise software to install.
🤝
Dedicated Onboarding Support
Our team helps with employee communication templates and enrollment strategy.
📖
Employee Training Materials
Ready-made onboarding guides, app walkthrough videos, and FAQ documents provided.
